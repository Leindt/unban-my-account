About us

We exist because
"contact support" doesn't work.

When a platform takes your account, the appeal form is a black hole. We built the direct path back — for creators, founders and brands whose livelihoods depend on these accounts.

Unbanmyaccount was founded in 2021 by a former Meta Trust & Safety operator and a tech-policy lawyer, after watching too many friends lose their livelihoods to opaque enforcement.

In the five years since, we've recovered 12,400+ accounts across every major platform — for individual creators, DTC founders, agencies, MCNs and Fortune 500 brands. We've built relationships with Trust & Safety teams at Meta, ByteDance, X, Google/YouTube, LinkedIn, Discord and Twitch that no public appeal form can replicate.

We charge a premium because the work is real. Every case absorbs a senior specialist, in-house legal review, identity documentation work, and direct platform liaison time. We turn down nearly one in five cases we evaluate — because saying yes to an unwinnable case is worse than saying no.

If we take your case, we have a 94% recovery rate. If we don't take it, we'll tell you why — for free.

Our principles

Four things we don't compromise on.

Honesty over revenue

We turn down 18% of cases. We refund every case we don't recover. We'd rather have a smaller business than a dishonest one.

Real humans, real expertise

Every case is owned by a senior specialist. Our case managers have prior careers in Trust & Safety, platform policy and tech law.

Security, not theatre

SOC 2 Type II. GDPR. NDAs on every engagement. We never ask for passwords. Documents deleted 30 days post-closure on request.

Boring sustainability

We don't burn relationships with platforms for one client. Our liaisons stay credible because every case we file is rock-solid.
