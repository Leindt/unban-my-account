About us

We exist because
"contact support" doesn't work.

When a platform takes your account, the appeal form is a black hole. We built the direct path back — for creators, founders and brands whose livelihoods depend on these accounts.

Unbanmyaccount started as a small recovery operation in 2021, after watching too many creators lose their livelihoods to opaque platform enforcement and useless appeal forms.

Five years on, we've recovered 12,400+ accounts across every major platform — for creators, DTC founders, agencies and brands. We don't file public appeals; we route cases through internal escalation channels that take hours, not weeks. That's the entire reason this works.

We charge a premium because the work is real and the relationships are expensive to maintain. We turn down nearly one in five cases we evaluate — saying yes to an unwinnable case (especially anything cyber-related) is worse than saying no.

If we take your case, we have a 94% recovery rate. If we don't take it, we tell you why — for free. Payment is crypto-only. Every order includes a 24-hour restoration insurance. Full refund if we can't recover.

Our principles

Four things we don't compromise on.

Honesty over revenue

We turn down 18% of cases. We refund every case we don't recover. We'd rather have a smaller business than a dishonest one.

Real humans, real expertise

Every case is owned by a senior specialist. Our case managers have prior careers in Trust & Safety, platform policy and tech law.

Security, not theatre

We never ask for your password. We never need login access. Encrypted communication via Telegram. Crypto-only payments mean no card details to leak.

Boring sustainability

We don't burn relationships with platforms for one client. Our liaisons stay credible because every case we file is rock-solid.
