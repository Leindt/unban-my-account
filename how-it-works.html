From banned to back online,
in four transparent steps.

No black box. No "trust us." Here's exactly what happens after you message us — and the timeline you can realistically expect.

Message @Hndrlndt with just four things: your username, follower count, a screenshot of the ban message, and the date the account was disabled. No password. No login access. Nothing else.

STEP 02

Eligibility check & fixed quote.

We review your case against our internal "winnable" criteria. If the ban reason is recoverable, we send you a fixed quote — starting at $3,500, scaled by account size and ban complexity. If it's not winnable, we tell you up front, for free.

  • Honest "we can't help" if we can't — every time
  • Fixed price quoted before any payment

  • Cyber-related bans (CSAM, hacking, terror) automatically declined

STEP 03

Crypto payment. Then we appeal immediately.

Once you confirm, we share the crypto wallet via Telegram. The second payment lands, the internal appeal begins. You receive updates whenever we hear back from platform reps — usually inside 24–48 hours, currently 2–5 days due to platform-wide delays.

  • Crypto only — BTC, ETH, USDT (TRC-20 / ERC-20)
  • Internal appeal channels, not the public queue
  • Status updates via Telegram as they come in
  • Refunds in crypto if recovery fails (full amount)

  • Refund (full) if re-disabled and we can't fix it again
