The process

From banned to back online,
in four transparent steps.

No black box. No "trust us." Here's exactly what happens after you submit your case — and the timeline you can expect.

STEP 01

Free case review.
Within 4 hours.

You submit your account handle, the ban message you received, and a short description. A senior specialist — not a chatbot — reads it and either accepts the case or honestly tells you it's not winnable.

  • No payment, no commitment, no upsell
  • 4-hour first response, 7 days a week
  • Honest "we can't help" if we can't — every time
  • Encrypted intake; we never ask for your password

STEP 02

Strategy & documentation. Assembled by a real team.

Once engaged, your case manager designs the recovery strategy specific to your ban type. Identity verification, legal documentation, business evidence — every piece prepared correctly the first time.

  • Ban-specific strategy (CG, impersonation, repeat violation, etc.)
  • Legal documentation reviewed by our in-house counsel
  • Identity & business evidence packaging
  • Counter-narrative drafted for direct platform contact

STEP 03

Direct platform liaison. Not the public queue.

Your case is escalated through our Trust & Safety contacts — relationships we've built over years. You'll receive daily progress updates with screenshots, response timestamps, and next steps.

  • Escalation through verified Trust & Safety contacts
  • Daily progress updates with verifiable evidence
  • Repeat appeals where allowed, parallel paths where possible
  • Senior liaison involvement for high-stakes cases

STEP 04

Recovery & hardening.
So it doesn't happen again.

Once your account is back, we don't just disappear. You get a 30-day monitoring & consultation period — we identify what triggered the original ban and ensure your account is hardened against future strikes.

  • 30-day post-recovery monitoring & alerts
  • Root-cause review & written remediation plan
  • Content audit & policy training session
  • Direct line to your case manager for 90 days

What's different

Why we win cases agencies can't.

Direct contacts, not forms

We have verified Trust & Safety contacts at every major platform — built over five years and 12,000+ cases. Your appeal doesn't sit in a public queue.

Legal documentation

In-house counsel reviews every case. Platforms respond differently when documentation arrives on legal letterhead with sworn statements where appropriate.

We say no a lot

We turn down ~18% of cases we evaluate. We'd rather lose your business than take money for a case we know we'll lose. That's why our 94% rate is real.
