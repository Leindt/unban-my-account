Why prices vary

Two things shape the final quote.

No surprises after engagement — we tell you the exact price before you pay a satoshi.

01

Account size

Follower count, monetization status and brand profile drive complexity. A 5k personal page is a different escalation than a 2M creator account or a verified brand.

02

Ban reason

"Easy" cases — first-strike CG flags, hacked accounts, impersonation — start at $3,500. Repeat-violation bans, ad-account holds and "final appeal denied" cases require more work and cost more.

cover
Included on every order

24-Hour Restoration Insurance

Every recovery comes with a 24-hour insurance window. If your account gets disabled again within that timeframe, we re-appeal and restore it at no extra cost. After 24 hours, the account is your responsibility.

Full refund

If we can't recover it, you get every cent back

If for any reason the account cannot be recovered after the appeal process, we issue a full refund. The only exceptions: successful recoveries (no refund) and cases where the buyer pulls out after the case is submitted to our internal reps.

Crypto only

Pay in crypto — BTC, ETH, USDT, more

We accept payment exclusively in cryptocurrency for speed, privacy and global reach. Wallet addresses are shared via Telegram after we confirm your case eligibility. No credit cards, no PayPal, no bank transfers.
